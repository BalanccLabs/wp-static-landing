Business Model

How Balancc Makes Money

Multiple proven revenue streams — not a "figure it out later" business.

1

Bill Payment Fees

Commission per transaction

Users pay utilities, subscriptions, rent, and airtime through Balancc. We earn on each transaction. High-frequency, recurring, and sticky.

2

Interest Spread

Vault deposits

User earns interest, we earn the spread from deposits held with custodial partners. Longer lock periods = higher spread.

3

Card Transaction Revenue

1.5-2.5% per transaction

Interchange fees on card payments. Secondary revenue stream from daily spending activity.

4

Float Income

Aggregate deposits

Interest earned on aggregate user deposits before payout. Scales with user growth.

Premium Features
