# Slide 4: How It Works

## The Balancc System

### 1. Money Comes In → Main Wallet
Your income lands in one place.

### 2. Auto-Allocate to Bags
Rent bag. Food bag. Transport bag. Each bag has a job and a limit.
- **When it's empty, you stop** — not your willpower, the system.
- **Bills auto-pay** so you never miss due dates.

### 3. Lock Savings in Vaults
- Set a lock period
- **Earn interest** (50% paid upfront, 50% at maturity)
- Remove temptation completely

### Result: Financial Clarity Without Daily Budgeting

### 🧾 1. Bill Payment Transaction Fees
**Commission per bill payment** — utilities, subscriptions, rent, airtime
- High-frequency, recurring transactions
- Users pay bills through Balancc → we earn on each transaction
- Sticky behavior: once set up, users don't switch

### 🏦 2. Interest Spread on Vaults
User earns **X%** → We earn spread from deposits held with custodial partners
- Longer lock periods = higher spread

# Key Talking Points for Investor Meetings

## Addressing Common Objections

### "How do you make money?"
**Answer:** We have 5 revenue streams:
1. **Bill payment fees** — commission on every utility, subscription, and rent payment
2. **Interest spread** on vault deposits
3. **Card transaction fees** (1.5-2.5%) on spending
4. **Float income** from aggregate deposits
5. **Premium features** (roadmap)

### "Isn't this just another budgeting app?"
**Answer:** No. Budgeting apps **track** what happened. Balancc **prevents** overspending by design. The system enforces discipline — when your food bag is empty, you can't spend food money on clothes. It's behavioral architecture, not expense logging.

### "Why will people use this over their bank?"
**Answer:** Banks give you one balance. That's the problem. Balancc gives your money **roles**. Plus: automated bill pay, interest-bearing vaults, and zero daily effort required.

### "What's your competitive moat?"
**Answer:** 
1. **Behavioral design** — built on psychology research, not just features
2. **Bill payment network** — recurring revenue from essential transactions users can't skip
3. **Trust** — licensed custodian partnership (Clearwater Trust)
4. **Network effects** — group savings features create virality

### "Can you scale this?"
**Answer:** Yes. Our infrastructure is cloud-native, our custodian partnership handles fund security, and our product is designed for self-service onboarding. The $200K gets us to [PLACEHOLDER: X] users and proves the model for a larger seed round.

## Product Features Roadmap

### Tier 1: High Impact (Next Priority)

**1. Salary Advance / Earned Wage Access**
- Partner with employers for early access to earned wages
- Fee per advance
- Fits mission: "Never let bills fail because payday is 3 days away"

**2. Group Vaults (Ajo/Esusu/Chama)**
- Rotating savings clubs — deeply cultural in Africa
- Users invite friends → viral growth
- Premium features for group admins
- Network effect: Each group = 5-10 users acquired together

**3. Shared Bags**
- Couples share a "Groceries" bag

- 2x users per relationship — natural viral growth

**4. Bill Splitting**

- Auto-request money from friends

### Tier 2: Crypto & Cards Expansion
