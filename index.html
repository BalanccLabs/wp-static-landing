Frequently Asked Questions

What is Balancc?

Balancc is a money management app that helps you organize spending, automate bills, and save consistently by giving your money structure. Instead of one big balance, Balancc gives your money clear roles — you decide what each part is for, and the system helps you stick to it automatically.

What's the difference between the Main Wallet, Bags, and Vaults?

Main Wallet: Your flexible money — spend or move it anytime with no restrictions or lock-ins.

Bags: Money with a job. Create bags for things like food, rent, transport, or utilities. Set clear limits and automate bill payments.

Vaults: For long-term discipline. Lock your money for a set period and earn interest. No temptation to dip in.

How does interest work with Vaults?

Vaults lock your money for a set period and earn interest. Half of the interest is paid upfront when you create the vault, and the rest is paid when the vault matures. This structure rewards discipline and helps you stay committed to your savings goals.

Is my money safe with Balancc?

Yes. Balancc does not directly hold customer funds. All funds are securely held with Fincra Technologies Limited. — a licensed financial custodial partner responsible for safeguarding customer balances. Your money is held in segregated accounts, and Balancc cannot move or use your funds without your instructions.

Who is Balancc for?

Balancc is built for people who earn a steady income, have rent, bills, subscriptions, or dependents, and want control without complexity. If you've ever said "I earn money, but I don't feel in control of it" — Balancc was built for you.

Do I need to track every expense manually?

No. With Balancc, you don't need to track every expense manually, think about money every day, or be "good" at budgeting. You only need to set it up once, and let the system do the rest. Balancc works quietly in the background — so you can focus on life.

What is Target Savings?

Target Savings lets you save automatically toward things that matter. Choose a goal, set how much and how often, and funds are locked for discipline. Interest accrues daily and is paid out when you complete the goal. Saving becomes predictable, intentional, and actually achievable.

Can I move money between Wallet, Bags, and Vaults?

Yes, you can freely move money between your Main Wallet and Bags. However, Vaults are designed for discipline — funds in Vaults are locked until maturity to help you stay committed to your long-term goals.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. Balancc believes in transparency — no surprises, no hidden fees, no unclear rules. All interest calculations are transparent, and you'll always know exactly what each feature costs before you use it.
